02. Methodology

The 5-stage studio process.

Transparent, rigorous, and milestone-governed. Here is how commissions evolve from hypothesis into shipped reality.

01

Hypothesis & Audit

We deconstruct the brand landscape, interrogate existing touchpoints, and define the core creative provocation.

02

Architectural Vision

Establishing the visual vocabulary, typography scales, material palette, and grid framework.

03

Rapid Prototyping

Building live working code and physical paper mockups to test tactile feel, motion latency, and responsive fidelity.

04

Precision Production

End-to-end implementation of all digital surfaces, pre-press print production, or physical fabrication blueprints.

05

Preservation & SLA

Delivery of design tokens, complete vector sources, documented code, and ongoing performance supervision.
